# Spec Delta

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Create key result
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Add measure" in the measures capability.

### Requirement: Fields depend on measure type
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Fields depend on measure type" in the measures capability.

### Requirement: Edit and delete key result
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Edit and delete measure" in the measures capability; projects and habits linked to a KR now serve its goal.

### Requirement: Effective KR deadline
**Reason**: Measures have no deadline; a goal's end date is its deadline.
**Migration**: Projects inherit their goal's end date (see projects). KR deadlines are dropped when key results become measures.

### Requirement: Numeric KR progress
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Numeric measure progress" in the measures capability.

### Requirement: Boolean KR progress
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Boolean measure progress" in the measures capability.

### Requirement: Habit KR progress
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Habit measure progress" in the measures capability.

### Requirement: Key result page
**Reason**: Measures have no page; they are shown and updated on the goal page.
**Migration**: See "Goal page" in the goals capability and "Record progress on the goal page" in the measures capability. Old KR URLs open the goal page (see app-shell).

### Requirement: Progress controls
**Reason**: Progress is recorded on the goal page.
**Migration**: See "Record progress on the goal page" in the measures capability.

### Requirement: Step size
**Reason**: Key results are replaced by measures of a goal.
**Migration**: See "Step size" in the measures capability.
